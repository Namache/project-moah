## Problem

The pack is longer than you want.

## Likely causes

- The detail level is Detailed.
- Many memories are set to *Always* visible.
- The task description or checkpoints are long.

## How to fix

1. In *AI context*, choose *Minimal* or a smaller size limit.
2. Change rarely needed memory to *Relevant only*.
3. Keep checkpoints short, and put long notes in files instead.
