Press **Ctrl+K** or **/** from anywhere, or click *Search or jump to* at the top of the sidebar. Type a few letters to jump to a page or project, or press Enter to search. Inside a project the first choice searches that project only; the next searches all projects, and results then show which project each belongs to.

You can search by words or by ID, such as CAMP-037. Archived items and replaced decisions are hidden unless you turn on *Include archived and history*.

Search results are for you to read. They are never sent to an agent automatically.
