Tasks are what you and your agents are building. Each has a status, a priority, acceptance criteria and a next action.

## Board and list

The *Board* has columns from Backlog to Done. Drag a card to change its status, or use the *Move to* menu on the card, which works from the keyboard. The *List* shows the same tasks grouped by status.

## Acceptance criteria

Criteria are a checklist of what must be true for the task to be finished. Agents use them to judge whether work is really done, so keep them specific.

## Links between tasks

A task can depend on another, block it, be part of it, or simply relate to it. A task that depends on unfinished work shows a *Waiting on a dependency* badge.

## Milestones

Group tasks under milestones, such as "Demo 0.1.1". Progress is counted from the tasks inside.
