## Problem

A decision shows as replaced and is no longer used.

## Likely causes

- A newer decision replaced it. This is expected: agents only receive the current one.

## How to fix

1. Open the decision to see which one replaced it and the full history.
2. If the replacement was a mistake, replace it again with the correct text.
