## Problem

You cannot find a task you created.

## Likely causes

- It was archived, or completed.
- You are looking in a different project.
- A milestone filter is active.

## How to fix

1. Use search, and turn on *Include archived and history*.
2. Use *All tasks* to look across projects.
3. Clear the milestone filter on the Tasks page.
