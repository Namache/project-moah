## Problem

A decision you expected is not in the context pack.

## Likely causes

- Its AI visibility is *Hidden* or *Manual only*.
- It is *Relevant only* and not linked to the task.
- The detail level or size limit left it out.
- It was replaced, or archived.

## How to fix

1. Open the decision and link it to the task, or set visibility to *Always*.
2. In *AI Context*, raise the detail level, or select manual-only memory to include it.
3. Lock and pin decisions that must never be missed.
